Terms of Service

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Overview: SplitUp provides an informational ledger calculation engine for friends and groups. SplitUp is not a bank or payment processor; settlements are recorded by mutual consent of the parties.

1. Use of the App

You agree to enter accurate expense records and acknowledge that all settlements require two-party confirmation before being marked as resolved.

2. Indian Paisa Mathematics

All splits are determined deterministically down to the exact paisa (1/100 INR) with integer arithmetic to eliminate rounding discrepancies.

3. Group & Trip Administration

Group creators maintain administrative permissions to manage members, but members cannot be removed while holding outstanding unsettled balances.

4. Contact

For support or legal inquiries, reach out to legal@splitup.app.
