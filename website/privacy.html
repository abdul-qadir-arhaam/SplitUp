Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Core Privacy Commitment: SplitUp does not sell your data. We collect only what is strictly necessary to calculate your shared bills, verify settlements, and send notifications.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, verified mobile phone number (+91), and optional email address.
  • Ledger & Expense Data: Amounts, categories, dates, descriptions, and participant shares created by you or your friends.
  • Private Nicknames: Private contact nicknames saved on your device remain strictly local and are never broadcasted to other participants.

2. How We Use Information

  • To compute net balances, pairwise debts, and simplified settlement graphs.
  • To send SMS verification codes and settlement confirmation prompts.
  • To maintain an audit trail for settlement safety.

3. Account Deletion & Anonymization

You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete Account. When deleted, your personal identity is replaced with "Deleted user" while preserving the mathematical integrity of historical expenses for your friends.

4. Contact Us

If you have questions about your privacy, contact our team at privacy@splitup.app.
